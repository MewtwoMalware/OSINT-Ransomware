# 🕵️ Ransomware OSINT Tracker

> **Track the actors. Follow the operations. Verify the sources.**

A curated collection of publicly available intelligence covering ransomware
groups, operations, arrests, sanctions, leaks, investigations, and major
developments.

Built for **OSINT researchers, threat-intelligence analysts, defenders,
security researchers, and anyone interested in tracking the ransomware
ecosystem.**

---

## 🔎 What is this?

Ransomware activity is reported across thousands of different sources:
security researchers, law-enforcement agencies, government announcements,
news outlets, incident reports, and threat-intelligence publications.

This project brings relevant public reporting together in one place.

The goal is to make it easier to:

- 🔍 Research ransomware groups
- 📅 Follow historical developments
- 🧩 Connect related public reporting
- ⚖️ Track arrests, investigations, and enforcement actions
- 💰 Track publicly reported sanctions
- 📰 Find original reporting and source material
- 🧠 Build a better understanding of ransomware operations

> **This repository is an OSINT research resource.**
> Information should always be verified against the original source.

# 📚 What is tracked?

Each group can contain publicly reported information relating to:

### 👥 Groups & Actors

- Group names
- Known aliases
- Publicly reported affiliations
- Group history
- Rebrands and successor groups

### 📰 Operations & Incidents

- Publicly reported attacks
- Major incidents
- Victim disclosures
- Significant operational developments

### ⚖️ Law Enforcement

- Arrests
- Indictments
- Investigations
- Infrastructure seizures
- International operations

### 💰 Sanctions & Enforcement

- Government sanctions
- Financial enforcement actions
- Court proceedings
- Regulatory actions
